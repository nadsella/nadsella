A plate can't make a vehicle look newer than it is. The year letter or age identifier sets the earliest registration date a plate can go on. Dateless plates carry no date, so they fit anything.

Before you buy

  • Faded characters are free choices. Pick any letter or number the DVLA has left.
  • Spacing is fixed by law. Moving the gap or using a stylised font to "fix" a spelling can mean an MOT fail, a fine of up to £1,000 and the DVLA withdrawing the number.
  • The DVLA withholds some combinations, including ones it finds offensive, and many plates are already owned. Use the DVLA link on each card to check what's for sale.
  • These suggestions follow the published format rules but aren't a guarantee a number exists or is available.
